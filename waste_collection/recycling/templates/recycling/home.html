How It Works

Every request moves through four clear stages, so you always know where things stand.

1

Submit a Request

Tell us what type of waste you have, how much, and when you'd like it picked up. Your request is instantly marked Submitted for Collection.

2

Pickup Accepted

Once our team accepts your request, it moves to Pending Pickup — collection is on the way.

3

Collected

When your waste is physically picked up, we mark it Collected.

4

Completed

Once your items are fully processed and recycled, the request is marked Completed.
